# Cauchy-Schwarz Inequality

> [!abstract] Definition (Theorem)
> Bounds inner products by the product of induced norms; tight iff vectors are collinear.

$$
|\langle \boldsymbol{u}, \boldsymbol{v} \rangle| \le \|\boldsymbol{u}\| \, \|\boldsymbol{v}\|
$$
